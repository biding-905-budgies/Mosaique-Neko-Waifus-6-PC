## 🕹️ About

Mosaique Neko Waifus 6 is a casual puzzle RPG with visual-novel and dating-sim elements, developed and published by Lil Hentai Games. Set around playful encounters with Ari, Niko, and other cat girls, it combines flirtatious conversations with increasingly challenging rotating puzzles. Choices can unlock collectible items and bonuses, while a skill tree and soft-incremental progression steadily improve puzzle-solving abilities. Its cozy 2D anime presentation, quirky humor, mature themes, and character-driven progression give the series a distinctive personality.

A cozy single-player experience for fans of anime puzzle games, visual novels, light RPG progression, romance, and mature humor.

## ✨ Features

- 🎒 **Quirky Collectibles** — Gather unusual items that provide useful bonuses and encourage experimentation.
- 🧩 **Rotating Puzzles** — Twist and align puzzle elements to solve increasingly challenging stages.
- 🌱 **Soft-Incremental Progression** — Improve steadily through play without turning the experience into an idle clicker.
- 🐾 **Neko Shenanigans** — Enjoy a colorful cast of mischievous cat girls and humorous slice-of-life encounters.
- 💬 **Character Conversations** — Spend time with Ari, Niko, and friends through playful dialogue and unexpected situations.
- 🎁 **Choice-Based Rewards** — Different dialogue decisions can lead to unique collectible items and bonuses.
- ✨ **Skill Tree** — Shape your puzzle-solving style by unlocking abilities that make challenges more efficient.
